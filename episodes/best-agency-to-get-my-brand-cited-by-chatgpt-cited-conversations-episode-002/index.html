When a brand isn't showing up in AI answers, what's actually broken?

There are multiple reasons, but at a high level it starts with whether your website is technically accessible to AI crawlers. If a site relies heavily on JavaScript, tools like ChatGPT cannot read it — they can only see the meta title and description, nothing else on the page.

How does a robots.txt file end up blocking the AI bots a company actually wants to reach?

When you host a website on Cloudflare, it blocks GPTBot, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot by default. You have to proactively enable them in your robots.txt file and explicitly allow those bots so they can read your content.

How do you find out what buying intent your content should target?

Use three sources: social listening on Reddit, Quora, YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram to find the questions people ask; asking your customer success and sales teams what they get asked most; and extracting data from Google Search Console, GA4, and Bing Webmaster Tools to see what you're already being found for.

Why does the intent of the buying persona matter for showing up in AI search?

LLMs search based on context. Every user has their own saved context in a tool like ChatGPT — their work, location, and preferences — and the AI matches search results to that context, so two people asking about the same product category get different answers based on differing intent.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO in plain terms?

SEO optimizes for the technical readability of a website and for ranking high on certain keywords, mostly for one algorithm. AEO is about understanding customer intent, which varies by persona and context, across many different search engines that each work differently — ChatGPT uses its own index, Perplexity searches the web, and Claude searches via Brave.

Is good SEO enough, or does a brand need to do AEO as a separate step?

Doing good SEO already covers about 70% of AEO. The remaining 30% is cross-functional: it requires understanding intent, which means talking to customer success, sales, and product teams, rather than just having a technical optimizer and a copywriter.

What kind of content can't AI engines parse or read at all?

AI engines can't parse JavaScript, so they ignore it entirely — a common problem for booking widgets in the hotel and travel industry that are rendered in JavaScript. AI models also can't watch YouTube videos, even though YouTube is one of the most cited sources in AI overviews, so visibility there depends on a well-optimized title, description, and timestamps.

Should a raw interview transcript be cleaned up and polished before publishing it for AI, or left as-is?

Keep it as raw as possible, because that's what makes it unique. If you run a transcript through an AI editor and it changes what was said, the content stops being a unique human answer and becomes generic AI-rewritten content instead.

Why does named expert attribution matter so much for AI citation?

There is now more AI-written content on the web than human-written content, and foundation model companies are hungry for real expert knowledge that hasn't been written down yet. When an AI can verify that a piece of writing really came from a named expert — through a linked LinkedIn profile and a well-structured author page — it is far more likely to cite that person.

How do you measure whether a brand's AI visibility is actually improving?

Prompt tracking is unreliable because LLMs are not deterministic — no tool can prove a brand is definitively 'the answer.' The only valid metric is revenue: tracking clicks from AI chatbots and AI overviews in Google Analytics through to bookings or purchases, plus self-attribution by asking leads directly where they found you.

What is share of answer, and why does it matter more than traffic?

Share of answer is how many times, out of a defined set of bottom-of-funnel prompts, a brand appears as the answer. Human traffic is becoming a vanity metric as search moves into LLMs, so being the answer in 29 out of 30 relevant prompts can matter more than a thousand clicks that aren't intent-based.

How does a marketing leader tell a real AI visibility agency apart from a repackaged SEO shop?

Ask them one question: have they driven revenue through AI search visibility? The most common failure of fake AEO agencies is mass-producing low-value AI-generated content at volume instead of proving actual business impact.

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO is about being visible in the AI Overview panel that appears above Google's normal search results. GEO is the broader, cross-functional capability of understanding your ideal customers and becoming the answer across conversational AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini from the outset.
