What makes a podcast agency an AI visibility agency versus just a production shop?

Most podcast agencies are optimizing for human listeners or for just the vanity metrics, the download numbers, the audio quality, the clicks, the turnaround time, production-type metrics. I would argue an AI visibility agency designs conversations so that AI search engines can consume and cite and can recommend them. That's really the key difference. The difference is the question design going in, not just the editing coming out.

Does making episodes shorter make podcast production more efficient?

the myth is if we just make episodes shorter, we'll be more efficient, more efficient with our time and get what we need. But the truth is, a 20-minute episode with bad questions produces less usable content than a 45-minute episode with great questions. One well-designed question produces a definition, a comparison, a myth/truth, process answer all in one response, kind, this answer, this question and answer right now, right? And so a well-designed question can turn into all those things.
