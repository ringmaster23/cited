What we look at

What makes a podcast library-ready

The library only holds answers a named person actually said, in their own words, traceable to a moment in the recording. The audit checks whether your show can supply them.

  1. Full conversations

    A catalog of full-length episodes where someone asks and someone answers. Trailers, promos, solo segments and re-airs don’t count toward the library.

  2. Questions that were actually asked

    Clear, answerable questions, and guests who respond at length. Each answer is kept whole, under the question the host really asked.

  3. Named speakers and a published transcript

    Everyone who speaks can be named, with a title and company. The episode has a transcript we can quote and link to the moment it was said.

  4. A feed we can follow

    A public RSS feed with your episodes, show notes and audio, so new conversations can be found and linked to.
