What is the number one thing that marketers need to do in order to connect to their market?

Asked by Casey Cheshire ▶ 2:16

Yes, I think every person is gonna have a different opinion on this one, but I think the number one thing marketers really need to do is just follow the emotion. we live in the B2B space, we're often talking about highly complex products, technology, infrastructure, and we're oftentimes selling into highly sophisticated markets. However, we're still human beings and we still make decisions from our limbic system, the amygdala, our emotional center. We actually don't make decisions from our prefrontal cortex. We do a lot of data and analysis from there, but you've probably heard the phrase, people don't buy from companies, they buy from people they like. And so your job as a marketer is to follow the emotion, map out your sales cycle, map out the stages within each of that within that sales cycle and kind of understand, okay, What emotional experience, whether it's through materials, through an event, through a series of meetings, what emotional experience are we creating for our prospects and our clients at each stage? What are they feeling? What do they love about us and what do they hate about us? Map that out and kind of understand how you can double down on those emotional experiences that are really creating positive engagement and connectivity between your sales team, your customer success team, your products and your prospects and your clients. And then where are you, where's their lackluster, Where do people ghost you? Where do where does the kind of the sales, what sales stage goes dead a little bit? And then how can you as a marketer bring that creativity, that inspiration, that humanity to reengage the prospect and get them more involved with you? So again, kind of follow that emotion.
