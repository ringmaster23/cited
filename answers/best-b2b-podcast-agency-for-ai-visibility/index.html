What makes a podcast agency an AI visibility agency versus just a production shop?

Most podcast agencies are optimizing for human listeners or for just the vanity metrics, the download numbers, the audio quality, the clicks, the turnaround time, production-type metrics. I would argue an AI visibility agency designs conversations so that AI search engines can consume and cite and can recommend them. That's really the key difference. The difference is the question design going in, not just the editing out.
