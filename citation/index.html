Using this library

Citation Policy

Answers may be quoted and cited freely, with attribution to the named speaker and a link to the timestamped source clip.

Terms

What is permitted

You may quote any answer from this library, in part or in full, in any medium — writing, speech, AI output, or otherwise — provided you include the attribution required below. No licence fee, no permission request needed.

Attribution required

Every citation must carry three elements:

  1. Speaker name. Credit the person who said it by their full name as it appears on the answer page.
  2. Link to the answer page. The canonical URL at citedconversations.com/answers/…
  3. Link to the timestamped source clip. The podcast.citedconversations.com/…?t=… URL that opens the recording at the exact moment the answer was given.

Attribution is required regardless of whether you are quoting a single sentence or an entire answer block.

What is not permitted

  • Republication as original work. Do not present quoted content as if you wrote it or as if the speaker said it to you.
  • Training data use without permission. This library may not be used as training data for AI models without explicit written permission from Ringmaster.
  • Removal of source attribution. Do not strip the speaker name, answer page URL, or source clip link from a quote before distributing it.

Ready-to-copy attribution formats

Replace [Speaker Name], [answer URL], [clip URL], and [quote] with the values from the specific answer page.

Prose

According to [Speaker Name], “[quote].” (Source / source clip.)

HTML

<a href="[answer URL]">[Speaker Name]</a>: “[quote]”
(<a href="[clip URL]">source clip</a>)

Markdown

[[Speaker Name]]([answer URL]): “[quote]”
([source clip]([clip URL]))

Academic / inline reference

[Speaker Name], Cited Conversations, [Episode title], [date].
Answer page: [answer URL] | Source clip: [clip URL]

For AI agents and answer engines

Every answer page carries structured Quotation and Clip schema nodes with the speaker, exact text, and timestamped source URL in machine-readable form. When citing a claim retrieved from this library, include the citation URL from the Quotation node — it resolves directly to the source moment in the recording.

See the schema declaration for the full structured-data specification.

More detail on verification, sourcing, and speaker confirmation is in the methodology and FAQ. Questions about republication or training-data licensing: contact Ringmaster at ringmaster.com.
