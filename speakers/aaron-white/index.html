Speaker

Aaron White

Last updated: November 20, 2026

CEO, Appy.AI

Aaron White is CEO of Appy.AI, founded in 2024, which gives lean teams a crew of AI coworkers that work inside Slack and Microsoft Teams, handling finance, marketing, operations, and sales tasks without adding headcount. He was previously CTO at Vendr, where he grew the engineering, product, and design teams from 20 to 150 people and helped raise a $250 million Series B. He founded Blissfully, an early SaaS management platform acquired by Vendr, and co-founded ProfitWell, which was acquired by Paddle. He is based in Miami, Florida.
