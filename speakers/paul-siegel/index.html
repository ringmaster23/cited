Paul Siegel

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Senior Account Executive, SmartKargo

Paul Siegel is a Senior Account Executive at SmartKargo, where he sells Delta's DeliverDirect, a service that ships e-commerce parcels on 4,000 Delta flights a day with door-to-door delivery in two to three days. He is based in Hoboken, New Jersey, and joined SmartKargo in October 2026. Paul has more than 13 years in enterprise software sales, including roles at Onna, Palantir Technologies, and Reveal. He holds a BA in marketing from Hofstra University and began his software sales career at SilverSky in late 2012.
