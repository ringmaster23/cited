Simon Wilhelm, Co-Founder and CEO of SCAILE.

Simon Wilhelm is Co-Founder and CEO of SCAILE, a content engine for AI search visibility headquartered in Hamburg, Germany. He co-founded SCAILE in January 2025 with August Gutsche and Julius Betzler and leads it as CEO. The company has worked with more than 40 companies across 14-plus industries, helping brands get cited and recommended by AI engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini through entity summaries, citation-ready content and machine-readable signals.
