Speaker

Kate Wood

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Chief Marketing Officer, Dataplor

Kate Wood is Chief Marketing Officer at Dataplor, a global location intelligence company. A four-time CMO, she has scaled companies from $10 million to $50 million in ARR and helped secure VC backing for the organizations she has led, with earlier CMO roles at Agio, Certified Tracking Solutions, and Lacuna Technologies, and a start in marketing at Morgan Stanley. She is also an ICF-certified executive coach, a graduate of Dr. Martha Beck's Wayfinder Life Coach Training program, and runs her own executive and life coaching practice, Wildly.
