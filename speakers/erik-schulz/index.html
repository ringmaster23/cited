Erik Schulz

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Founder, Newbridge Advisory

Erik Schulz is the founder of Newbridge Advisory, which uses AI-enabled analytics to turn thousands of customer reviews into insights and specific actions that help companies grow faster and retain more customers. Before founding Newbridge Advisory in 2025, he spent more than twenty years in go-to-market, strategy and product roles, including seven years at Gartner, where he led advisory operations, and earlier stops at McKinsey & Company, Medidata, Calero, SAP and eBay Enterprise.
