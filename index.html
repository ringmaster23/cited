How it works

Source-linked answers from named speakers

Expert conversations are recorded as a podcast, then broken into question-shaped answer pages where every claim is attributed to a named speaker and links to the precise playable moment in the source recording.

  • Answers come from recorded, attributed conversations — not generated text.
  • Every claim links to its timestamp in the source episode.
  • Speakers are confirmed at intake by the show's producer.
  • Pages are spot-audited against source audio before publishing.
